WeRecruit4You is an AI-powered recruitment matching platform. Candidates upload their CV, companies upload their job descriptions, and our AI finds the right match. Candidates and companies can subscribe to unlock the full power of AI matching and personalized recruiting support. By using this chat you agree to our processing of your data for recruitment purposes.

Before you upload — how we use your data

When you upload your CV or job description, our AI will screen it against real data — live open positions and active candidates in our database. If there is a strong match, we will tell you right away.

If there is no match today, we may keep your data for future screenings — and that works in your favor: new positions and new candidates arrive constantly, so a match that doesn't exist today may exist next month. If that happens, we will contact you.

Your data is handled confidentially and used solely for recruitment matching. You may request its deletion at any time at contact@werecruit4you.pro.
